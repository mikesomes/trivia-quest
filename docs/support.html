Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated gameplay data at any time from within the app:

Profile tab → Settings (gear icon) → Delete Account

Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Reporting a question

If a trivia question looks wrong, offensive, or broken, flag it directly from the question screen in the app — flagged questions are reviewed and removed from rotation.
