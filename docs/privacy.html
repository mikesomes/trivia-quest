Privacy Policy

Trivia Quest  ·  Last updated: April 24, 2026

Who we are

Trivia Quest is a mobile trivia game developed and operated by Mike Somers. Questions in this app mean contact at somersm8@gmail.com.

What data we collect

When you install Trivia Quest, we automatically create an anonymous account for you — no email address, name, or phone number is required or collected.

  • Anonymous user ID — a random UUID generated on first launch, used to save your scores and progress.
  • Display name — a name you choose (or a random default). Shown on the leaderboard.
  • Gameplay data — rounds played, answers submitted, XP earned, items purchased with in-game coins, and leaderboard scores.
  • Crash and error reports — if the app crashes, a report is sent to Sentry (sentry.io) containing the stack trace and basic device information (OS version, device model). No personal data is included.

We do not collect: real name, email, location, contacts, camera/microphone input, or any payment information.

How we use your data

  • To save your game progress and display it on the leaderboard.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability (Sentry crash reports).

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or third-party marketers.

Where data is stored

Gameplay data is stored in Supabase (supabase.com), a PostgreSQL database hosted on AWS. Data may reside in the United States or European Union depending on your project region.

Crash reports are processed by Sentry (sentry.io), hosted in the United States.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained for as long as you use the app. You can permanently delete your account and all associated gameplay data at any time from within the app:

Profile tab → Settings (gear icon) → Delete Account

Deletion is immediate and irreversible. Your anonymous user ID, display name, scores, and all gameplay history are permanently removed.

Children's privacy

Trivia Quest is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Advertising and tracking

Trivia Quest contains no advertisements and does not use any cross-app tracking or advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID).

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

Questions or requests (including data deletion requests): somersm8@gmail.com
